Speech Enhancement App

Problem?

Contact centers maintain dedicated quality assurance and speech coaching teams to monitor calls and score agents on clarity and tone. Realistically only fraction of thousand calls are reviewed. Most interactions go unexamined, making the feedback loop slow and the path to training a good agent both slow and expensive. The worst part, the attrition rate is high! People leave and join everyday. So the whole process can just be a waste of time.

What can we do?

We can automate QA but it doesn't solve anything, just increases the volume of actions and teams will be drowned with training requirements tailored for each agent.
