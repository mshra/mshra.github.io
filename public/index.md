# Aaryan Mishra

Aaryan Mishra is a software engineer and Delhi Technological University graduate. He is currently a founding engineer at [Arctan](https://www.linkedin.com/company/arctan-ai/home/), working on production Voice AI systems.

His work includes:

- [Eigen voice isolation](https://aaryanmishra.com/voice-isolation/index.md), a model processing more than 100,000 minutes of noisy audio each day and integrated with [Pipecat](https://docs.pipecat.ai/api-reference/server/services/audio-filters/arctan).
- SDKs and a Rust library for speech-model inference.
- A Go-based WebRTC inference pipeline with 83% lower latency, migrated to LiveKit for reliable scaling.
- The [Eigen developer platform](https://getarctan.com/eigen).
- An [on-device speech enhancement application](https://aaryanmishra.com/speech-app/index.md) used in more than 20 BPOs and call centers across India.

Aaryan enjoys messy problems, ambitious people, and the pursuit of greatness.
