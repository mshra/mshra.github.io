01

Problem

Contact centers and BPOs maintain dedicated quality assurance and speech-coaching teams to monitor calls and score agents on clarity, tone, and pace. The cost is significant, yet these teams can realistically review only a small fraction of the thousands of calls handled each day.

Most interactions go unexamined, making the feedback loop slow—and the path to training a good call agent both slow and expensive.

02

This is where Arctan comes in.

Instead of waiting for feedback, Arctan enhances every agent’s voice as they speak, on every call, from day one. The speech-to-speech model operates at the system level, adjusting clarity, tone, and pace transparently.

The customer hears the agent at their best—not the version that might emerge only after months of coaching.

03

Features

  • Pitch stabilization
  • Timbre enhancement
  • Noise cancellation
  • Gender adaptive

04

Gender adaptive

Arctan detects vocal characteristics and routes each voice to the enhancement model best suited to its qualities.

End-to-end latency

Latency can make or break a conversation. Audio drops are often tolerable; delay is not. It causes people to speak over one another and turns natural pauses into frustrating gaps.

Every part of the audio pipeline was therefore optimized to keep the conversation feeling immediate.
