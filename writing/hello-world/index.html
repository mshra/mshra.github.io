Hello, World

January 15, 2024

An introduction to this site, why I built it, and what you can expect to find here.

Every site needs a first post, so here we are.

I’ve been meaning to carve out a small corner of the internet for myself for a while now. Not a social media profile, not a LinkedIn page — something more permanent. A place where the writing doesn’t disappear into an algorithmic feed and the photos don’t get compressed into oblivion.

Why now?

Partly because I finally got tired of saying “I should really set up a personal site” and not doing it. Partly because I’ve been writing more — notes, observations, half-formed thoughts — and it felt wasteful to keep them locked in a notes app nobody else would ever read.

There’s also something I find appealing about the old-school personal website. Before social platforms homogenized everything, people built their own spaces with their own rules. The design reflected the person. The content wasn’t optimized for engagement. You’d stumble across someone’s site and feel like you’d actually learned something about who they were.

I wanted that.

What to expect

I’ll write about things I find interesting: software, design, books, the occasional observation about the world. No editorial calendar, no content strategy. Just things worth writing down.

If something here resonates with you, feel free to reach out. I’m not hard to find.

— Aaryan
