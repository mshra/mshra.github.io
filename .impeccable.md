## Design Context

### Users

The portfolio serves a broad professional and personal network: hiring teams,
technical peers, collaborators, and people who want a concise sense of Aaryan's
work and point of view. Visitors should be able to understand the person behind
the work without navigating through unnecessary interface chrome.

### Brand Personality

Thoughtful, warm, and understated. The interface should feel calm and assured,
with an editorial voice that is personal without becoming decorative or
precious.

### Aesthetic Direction

A minimal, light, text-first portfolio inspired by the restraint and generous
whitespace of https://ws-kj.github.io/ and https://willmanidis.com/. Typography,
measure, and rhythm carry the design. Avoid generic portfolio cards, glossy
effects, dark-mode neon, gradients, oversized interface elements, and visual
decoration without meaning.

### Design Principles

1. Build hierarchy with type, weight, and space before adding decoration.
2. Keep prose comfortably readable with generous leading and a narrow measure.
3. Prefer one distinctive, versatile type family over competing font pairings.
4. Make links, focus states, zoom, and contrast accessible by default.
5. Preserve quiet negative space and let every visible element earn its place.
